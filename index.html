How I coach

  1. Assess first. Every client starts with a consultation and screening, so the plan fits their goals, history and limits.
  2. Technique before load. Clean form first, then we progress. That comes straight from my biomechanics and physio clinic training.
  3. Progress you can see. I log every session, so we both know exactly what's improving.
  4. Meet people where they are. First-timer or 60+, same principles, scaled to the person.

My own training

I don't coach from a textbook alone. I've been lifting for over 8 years and compete in powerlifting, winning the 2026 WRPF-CAN Tested national title at 90 kg (Junior and Open).

Almost everything I put in a client's program, I've done myself, so I know how it feels, what it takes to recover from, and how to fit it around work and school.
